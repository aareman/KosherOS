A good family computer should be useful as well as protected. Filtering is built into KosherOS. So are the tools to make the computer your family’s own.

Separate accounts, settings you choose, and groups your family names. Start with the strict default, then give each person the access they need.

Set daily limits and allowed hours when you want them. Each person can see their rules and remaining time in My Filter.

Privacy built in

The content filter runs locally on your computer, without a separate filtering account or subscription. Its activity view records what it blocks, not a history of everything you read.

What the parent sees

The family, all in one place.

Choose what applies to each person. Answer a request for a blocked site. See whether the filter is working. KosherOS Admin brings those everyday decisions together, with an optional guardian password for changes that reduce protection.

שמירת עיניים · shmiras einayim

Filtering is part of the computer.

Web pages, pictures, language and video have their own settings. In filtered modes, KosherOS checks content locally as well as checking the address. See what is covered and where the limits are.

  • Choose filtered internet, approved sites only, or no internet for an account.
  • Judges a page nobody has ever catalogued, by what is written on it.
  • Covers the figure in a picture instead of blanking the site around it.
  • Looks inside a video and an animation a few frames at a time, not only at its cover.
  • Replaces bad language on a page that is otherwise fine to read.
  • Holds YouTube to its limits inside the app, not on top of it.
  • Blocks ads and trackers at the resolver for every account, the way a Pi-hole does.

Around it

Useful apps.
Room to discover.

A store with choices for parents. Start with approved apps, or open an account to the broader Flathub store with content-rating safeguards. Block categories or individual apps, and keep installed apps up to date from the Store.

Search that respects the filter. Built-in search filters results using the account’s settings. Approved-sites-only accounts can search within their allowed sites, including ready-made lists for Torah study and email.

A window for the person being filtered. My Filter is a read-only app on every account that says, in plain language, what applies to you. The rules and time limits are there to read, and a blocked page offers a way to ask a parent for access.
